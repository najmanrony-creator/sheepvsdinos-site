FAQ

How do I play?
Tap a lane to walk Ronald there, then tap the field to throw food.

Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device only. Deleting the app erases it.

Are there in-app purchases?
No. Wool is earned by playing.

Does it need internet?
No, it works offline.
