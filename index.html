Sheep vs. Dinos

Sheep happens!

The game

The dinos are hungry. The sheep are nervous. Help Ronald the farmer keep the dinos fed so his flock makes it home to the safe pen. A friendly one-thumb arcade game for the whole family, with 61 levels, 12 boss dinos, and English and Portuguese. No account needed.

Free on the App Store for iPhone.
