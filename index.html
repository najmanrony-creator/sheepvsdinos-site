Sheep vs. Dinos

The game

The dinos are hungry. The sheep are nervous. Help Ronald the farmer keep the dinos fed so his flock makes it home to the safe pen. A friendly one-thumb arcade game for the whole family, with 61 levels, 12 boss dinos, and English and Portuguese. Works fully offline.
